[Google Maps](http://maps.google.com/) continues to include some higher resolution images (if not maps) for some parts of the planet. I was able to find the [place where I used to live in Santiago](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&hl=en&q=santiago+chile&ll=-33.432834,-70.640792&spn=0.002986,0.005831&t=k) at the level of buildings and houses. And here is [where I used to study](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&hl=en&q=santiago+chile&t=k&ll=-33.569804,-70.631586&spn=0.002856,0.005831) Just have a look at the centre of the pictures.

Distances between Christchurch places where I have lived at some point of my life (obtained using Geobyte’s [city distance tool](https://web.archive.org/web/20060927212141/http://www.geobytes.com/CityDistanceTool.htm)):

- Hobart, Australia: 2442 km.
- Palmerston North, New Zealand: 431 km.
- Valdivia, Chile: 8647 km.
- Mendoza, Argentina: 9487 km.
- Valera, Venezuela: 12876 km.
- Los Teques, Venezuela: 13255 km.
- Santiago, Chile: 9351 km.
- Concepcion, Chile: 8930 km.

It should be relatively easy to tag the images with every conceivable story that one is blogging about, although a bit time consuming. However, for most places I write about, imagery resolution is too broad for anything useful. I am sure it is just a matter of waiting a couple of years to sort out this issue.
