I was asking about introductory Forest Mensuration and Forest Growth books and got:

## Forest mensuration

1. Forest measurements by Avery and Burkhart (fifth or latest edition)  
2. Tree and forest measurement by PW West (metric units!).  
3. Prodan et al. 1997. [Mensura forestal](https://repositorio.iica.int/handle/11324/15038)   
4. Burkhart, HE, TE Avery, and BP Bullock. 2019. [Forest Measurements](https://www.waveland.com/browse.php?t=668). Sixth edition. Waveland Press 

## Forest growth

1. [Modeling forest trees and stands](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-90-481-3170-9) by Burkhart and Tomé 2012.   
2. Forest stand dynamics by Hans Pretzsch
