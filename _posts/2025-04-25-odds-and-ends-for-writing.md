A few things that I need to remember when writing posts for this site:

* Creating a basic post template in Jekyll with `jekyll-compose` as in
`bundle exec jekyll compose "New Post Title"`, which will create an empty
file named date-new-post-title with basic metadata in the `_posts`
directory.
More syntax details [in this page](https://github.com/jekyll/jekyll-compose).
* [El fondo sin fin de los bancos de cultura digital](https://www.eldiario.es/cultura/fondo-bancos-cultura-digital_1_12226268.html)
listing lots of "archivos históricos, artísticos o de la cultura pop, 
hemerotecas y fototecas gratuitas que nos pertenecen".
